Support

Help deciding, saving, and planning meals together.

Quick help

  • Add home meals and restaurants in Meal Library.
  • Tap a suggestion to save it to Meal History.
  • Use Group Pick to invite others and vote Yes, Maybe, or No.
  • Add recipe ingredients directly to Grocery List.
  • Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen to request a new six-digit code.

Contact support

Email EatPick.support@gmail.com. Never send your password or six-digit recovery code.

Delete your Eat Pick account

For immediate deletion, open Profile inside Eat Pick and choose Delete account permanently. The app will ask for confirmation before deleting the account and its associated personal data.

If you no longer have the app, send an account-deletion request from the email address registered to your Eat Pick account. Send the request to EatPick.support@gmail.com. We will verify the request and complete it within seven days.

Limited records will be retained only when required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance, as explained in the Privacy Policy.
