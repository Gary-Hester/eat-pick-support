EAT PICK

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Eat Pick helps individuals and households decide what to eat, save meal and restaurant ideas, vote together, maintain recipes and grocery lists, and track food spending.

Information Eat Pick processes

Eat Pick processes information you provide or create while using the app, including:

  • Account email address and profile display name
  • Household and group membership
  • Saved meals, restaurants, favorites, exclusions, and meal history
  • Group Pick choices, votes, and results
  • Recipes, ingredient lists, and grocery-list items
  • Monthly food targets and food-expense entries
  • Saved restaurant details you choose to enter

How information is used

This information operates the app, keeps information available across devices, generates meal suggestions, supports household collaboration, calculates group results, and displays food-spending summaries.

Sharing

Household and Group Pick information is available to authenticated people who belong to the same household or group. Eat Pick does not sell personal information. Supabase provides authentication and database services and processes app information on Eat Pick’s behalf.

Location and maps

The current version does not continuously track precise device location and does not enable a paid live-map provider. Saved restaurant details are used only for features the user chooses to use.

Retention and deletion

Users can remove many saved items inside the app and can use Delete account to permanently delete their account. People who no longer have the app can use the external request process on the Eat Pick Support page. Limited records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Security

Eat Pick uses authenticated access and database row-level security to restrict household and group information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

Eat Pick is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Eat Pick changes. The latest update date will appear above. Questions about this policy can be sent to EatPick.support@gmail.com.
